## 1. INTRODUCTION


### 1.1. PRESENT SITUATION:

Leak on low pressure condensate line was fixed by TLR. Repair on-the-run is impossible, this
scope transferred to TA24 scope of work.


### 1.2. PROPOSED SOLUTION:

Dismantle clamp and replace damaged pipe section.


### 2. SCOPE OF WORK

· CONTRACTOR shall strictly follow all relevant TCO Safety Instructions in the
progress of works, all of which are readily obtainable from the TCO Intranet.

· CONTRACTOR shall follow all Quality Management (QM) requirements
designated in the Quality Control package.


#### 2.1. PRELIMINARY SITE WORK:

2.1.1. Prior to the start of the mechanical activities, perform wall thickness testing at TP-001
and TP-002 to confirm that wall thickness is adequate for welding. Results shall be
reviewed and approved by the Fixed Equipment Reliability (FER) Unit Inspector.

NOTE: Perform the wall thickness test by marking out and recording a matrix as
directed by the TCO FER Unit Inspector.


#### 2.2. SITE WORK:

2.2.1. Perform relevant job safety assessments (PPHA & JSA) and obtain required Permits-
to-Work (PTWs).

2.2.2. Demarcate work area as necessary and erect scaffolding for access.

NOTE: TCO Operations shall isolate, depressurize, drain and ready for Hot Work,
Equipment and Piping associated with this Project, if and as applicable, and shall hand
it over to CONTRACTOR; fully accessible, safe and Locked-Out-Tagged-Out
(LOTO) as scheduled and agreed between TCO Operations and CONTRACTOR.

2.2.3. Remove all cladding and insulation to the extent required.

2.2.4. Cold Cut and remove existing piping shown on attached sketch L-SK-001.

2.2.5. Bevel cut ends of existing piping at the tie-in points TP-001 and TP-002 for field
welding as shown on attached sketch L-SK-001.

2.2.6. Inspect the prepared weld bevel ends 100% VT and 100 % MT/PT for defects.

NOTE: Perform inspection of weld bevels and nipo-flange landings PRIOR to
welding.

<!-- PageBreak -->

2.2.7. Install piping as specified on attached sketch L-SK-001. For field welds use TCO-
approved WPSs indicated on attached sketch L-SK-001.

2.2.8. Perform NDE of field welds, as specified on attached sketch L-SK-001.

2.2.9. Perform surface preparation and touch up field welds and any other coating damages
in accordance with TES COM-SU-4743-TCO and coating systems per TES COM-SU-
5191-TCO.

2.2.10. All QM piping passport documentation (A-category Checklists) to be verified and
signed by TCO-approved QM Inspector prior to PSSR, but not later than 24 hours after
mechanical scope completion.

2.2.11. Advise DE T/A Engineer at ext. 3972 about work completion and invite all necessary
people to PSSR.

2.2.12. Job Pack Coordinator shall submit the completed PIC check-list to Operations
department after being signed by Quality Management group. (Form is provided by
QM Dept.).

2.2.13. Conduct PSSR and close out A-category punch points.

2.2.14. Following PSSR reinstate / install insulation and cladding, as per TES IRM-SU-1381-
TCO and as specified on attached sketch L-SK-001.

2.2.15. Close out all B-category punch points and remove associated scaffolding.

2.2.16. Clean up the work area and demobilize from site.


#### 2.3. AFTER COMPLETION OF WORK

2.3.1. Perform service test.

2.3.2. Final as-built package (including B-category Checklist) shall be submitted to TCO QM
group within 2 weeks after completion of the whole scope of works.

2.3.3. Return excess material to TCO Warehouse.
