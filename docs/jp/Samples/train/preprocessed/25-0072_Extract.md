## PRESENT SITUATION:

. There is leak in the pipe section 63-9100-TL-1992-1 1/2 -150H22-HCW5. TLR was installed WO
1722132 TAG 23150.


## PROPOSED SOLUTION:

. Replace damaged section of 63-9100-TL-1992-1 1/2 -150H22-HCW5 line due to leak.


## SCOPE OF WORK

. Contractor shall strictly follow all relevant TCO Safety Instructions in the progress of works, all
of which are readily obtainable from the TCO Intranet.

. Contractor shall follow all Quality Management (QM) requirements designated in the Quality
Control package.


## SHOP WORK:

· Withdraw materials required for this Job Pack from TCO Warehouse according to Material
Request(s).

· Prefabricate pipe spools and pipe supports, as per isometric drawing(s) 062-0200-LLL-ISO-
20483-01. Use TCO-approved Welding Procedure Specification(s) (WPSs) indicated on isometric
drawing.

· Perform non-destructive examination (NDE) of shop welds, as specified on isometric drawing
062-0200-LLL-ISO-20483-01.


## SITE WORK:

· Perform relevant job safety assessments (PPHA & JSA) and obtain required Permits-to-Work
(PTWs).

· Demarcate work area as necessary.

NOTE: TCO Operations shall isolate, depressurize, drain and ready for Hot Work, Equipment and
Piping associated with this Project, if and as applicable, and shall hand it over to CONTRACTOR;
fully accessible, safe and Locked-Out-Tagged-Out (LOTO) as scheduled and agreed between TCO
Operations and CONTRACTOR.

· Remove all cladding and insulation to the extent required.

. Arrange with Zone Maintenance to isolate, LOTO and remove electric trace heating and
instruments from associated piping.

NOTE: CONTRACTOR to match mark and record location prior to cutting and removal to aid in
reinstatement of heat tracing.

. Install temporary supports.

. Cut, unbolt and remove existing piping and pipe supports shown on "Destruct Detail" of
isometric drawing(s) 062-0200-LLL-ISO-20482-01.

<!-- PageBreak -->

· Install piping as specified on isometric drawing 062-0200-LLL-ISO-20483-01. For field welds use
TCO-approved WPSs indicated on isometric drawing(s).

· Perform NDE of field welds, as specified on isometric drawing 062-0200-LLL-ISO-20482-01.

· Perform surface preparation and touch up field welds and any other coating damages in
accordance with TES COM-SU-4743-TCO and coating system(s) per TES COM-SU-5191-TCO
specified on drawings.

· Arrange with Zone Maintenance to reinstate / install related electric trace heating and
instruments.

. All QM piping passport documentation (A-category Checklists) to be verified and signed by
TCO-approved QM Inspector prior to PSSR, but not later than 24 hours after mechanical scope
completion.

· Advise Mursal Rauan / Ramazanov Nurbulat DE TA26 SGP/SGI Mechanical Engineers at ext.
4259 about work completion and invite all necessary people to PSSR.

. Job Pack Coordinator shall submit the completed PIC check-list to Operations department after
being signed by Quality Management group. (Form is provided by QM Dept.).

· Conduct PSSR and close out A-category punch points.

· Following PSSR reinstate / install insulation and cladding, as per TES IRM-SU-1381-TCO and as
specified on isometric drawing.

· Close out all B-category punch points and remove associated scaffolding.

. Clean up the work area and demobilize from site.


## AFTER COMPLETION OF WORK:

· Perform service test.

· Final as-built package (including B-category Checklist) shall be submitted to TCO QM group
within 2 weeks after completion of the whole scope of works.

· Return excess material to TCO Warehouse.
